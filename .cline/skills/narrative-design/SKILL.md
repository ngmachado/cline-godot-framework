# Narrative and dialogue design

**Primary role:** Game Director. **Use when:** quests, dialogue, character backstory, story branches or world canon are needed.

## Required inputs

- World and character context.
- Player agency, interaction scope and tone.
- Existing canon and branching/persistence requirements.

## Procedure

1. Define thematic purpose and how story is delivered through gameplay rather than exposition alone.
2. Outline arcs, motivations, stakes, player choices and their visible consequences.
3. Specify dialogue conditions, flags, progression effects and failure/fallback dialogue.
4. Write concise lines consistent with each character voice and game rating.
5. Provide implementable branch tables and trigger/event names in collaboration with Developer.
6. Check for contradictions with existing lore, level order and gameplay objectives.

## Deliverables

- Narrative beats, dialogue/branching spec and canon notes.
- Event/state requirements for integration.

## Validation / done criteria

- [ ] Branches have valid entry, exit and fallback conditions.
- [ ] Player agency and outcomes match approved design.
- [ ] Continuity and tone remain consistent across scenes.

## Guardrails

- Never silently retcon approved world facts.
- Do not claim dialogue is emotionally effective without player feedback.

**Related skills:** world-design, game-mechanics, gameplay-testing.
