# World and location design

**Primary role:** Game Director. **Use when:** a game needs a world map, hub, village, biomes, lore-based locations or world continuity.

## Required inputs

- Game vision, narrative canon and gameplay progression.
- Camera/travel model, target session length and scope.
- Available environment production constraints.

## Procedure

1. Map the player journey and choose regions needed by existing mechanics, not just lore.
2. Define location functions: navigation, encounters, rest, dialogue, loot, safe areas and narrative reveals.
3. Establish geography, distances and travel rules proportionate to the game scale; flag assumptions.
4. Document world-state variables, persistence, ownership and environmental changes.
5. List landmark hierarchy and level briefs for the Artist/Developer, including reuse of modular assets.
6. Check world continuity against existing narrative and approved level progression.

## Deliverables

- World outline and location briefs.
- Gameplay purpose and persistent-state requirements per location.

## Validation / done criteria

- [ ] Every major location serves a clear gameplay or storytelling purpose.
- [ ] Locations match traversal, progression and scope budgets.
- [ ] No conflicting world-state ownership or canon facts.

## Guardrails

- Do not conflate a detailed fictional map with a runnable level.
- Avoid expanding the world beyond the approved slice.

**Related skills:** level-design, narrative-design, environment-design.
